BENBEN SMARTCARS

Founded in 2016, BenBen SmartCars is one of Nairobi's most trusted motor dealers. Located at Industrial Area, Nairobi, we've grown from a small lot of 20 vehicles to Kenya's most comprehensive automotive hub.

We specialize in quality imported vehicles from Japan, UK, and UAE — all thoroughly inspected, NTSA-compliant, and ready for Kenyan roads. Our financing partnerships with Equity, KCB, Co-op Bank and Stanbic make car ownership accessible to every Kenyan.
